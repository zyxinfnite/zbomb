THE CITY HAS FALLEN

A real 3D first-person city survival game built with Three.js and WebGL. Track enemies on the tactical radar, build custom weapons, swing across the arena with a grappler, dash through danger, and beat your personal best.

STORY MODE: 8 chapters across 100 waves. FREEPLAY: endless waves with no final ending. Explore the ruined city, fight hordes and bosses, earn Zan-bits, and use the weapon workshop.

INCIDENT 07 — BLACKOUT: Three nights ago, Nova Systems lost the city grid after its underground Echo project began repeating voices from sealed rooms. The flashlight, sensors, remote doors, cameras, and field computer now use independent power systems, so one device can never shut the others down. Search the ruined city and read recovered incident files to learn what escaped.

NEW: Complete rotating field contracts for bonus Zan-bits: headshots, boss hunts, scavenging, wave clears, and survival. Press J to reroll a contract for 80 Zan-bits.

DIFFICULTY (changing it restarts the run)

Chromebook ready: use Chrome, keep internet on, and choose LOW graphics if it runs slowly. Use WASD to move, mouse/trackpad to look, click to shoot, Space to jump, T to grapple, X to dash, H to heal, and Esc to pause. Press P for the handheld access device; use E at the field computer to view it on the terminal.

OFFLINE PLAY: first save the official Three.js r160 engine as three.min.js in the same folder as this HTML. Use Download_ThreeJS_r160.html once while online. Then the original 3D game works without internet. If Chrome asks, allow mouse lock.
